Wörtli

First words in Swiss German, for toddlers. Spoken by a Swiss dad.

Picture books and talking toys are easy to find in English or German. In Swiss German, there are hardly any. So I recorded my own for my son: 351 everyday words in 19 categories, from animals and food to colors, family and things to do.

  • 🟦 Plättli: tap a picture, hear the word
  • 🦜 Papagei: hear the word, say it yourself, hear yourself back
  • Huge tap targets, no reading needed.
  • Calm colors, gentle animations, one voice.
  • No scores, stars, levels or timers. Every try counts.
  • No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no internet, no data collected.

Which Swiss German?

There are many Swiss German dialects, and Wörtli speaks one of them: mine, from Aargau. If your family says a word differently, that's part of the fun. Teach them both.

For grown-ups

Papagei uses the microphone only while it's your child's turn to speak. The recording stays in the phone's memory just long enough to play it back, and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Wörtli plays nicely with Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility), so little hands stay inside the app. Start it on the start screen and your child can switch between Plättli and Papagei. Start it inside one and they stay there.
