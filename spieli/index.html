Spieli

Calm little games for toddlers. No ads, no scores, no noise.

I made Spieli for my two little ones, because most kids' apps felt like slot machines. It's a handful of simple, quiet games for little hands that are still learning:

  • 🐢 Animal puzzle: drag big animals into their shapes
  • 🫧 Bubbles: pop soft bubbles as they float up
  • 🙈 Memory: find the matching pairs
  • 🎨 Color mixing: red and yellow make orange
  • 🪣 Buckets: sort colored balls into their buckets
  • Huge tap targets, forgiving touch. Palms and sloppy taps are fine.
  • Soft colors, gentle animations, quiet sounds.
  • No scores, stars, levels, timers or streaks.
  • No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no internet, no data collected.
